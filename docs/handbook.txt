NIMBUS COFFEE CO. EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Work Hours
Baristas work shifts of 6 or 8 hours. Shift swaps must be approved by a shift lead at least 24 hours in advance. Employees get a 15-minute paid break for every 4 hours worked.

Vacation Policy
Full-time employees earn 1.5 vacation days per month, up to 18 days per year. Unused vacation days roll over, but no more than 5 days can carry into the next year. Vacation requests must be submitted two weeks in advance through the Brewline app.

Employee Perks
All employees receive one free drink per shift and 40% off whole-bean coffee. After one year of employment, staff can join the Latte Art Academy, a paid 3-day training course held every spring in Halifax.

Dress Code
Staff wear the green Nimbus apron, closed-toe non-slip shoes, and dark pants. Hats must be Nimbus-branded. Visible jewelry should be limited to small studs and a single ring.

Equipment
Each store uses the Aurora X2 espresso machine. If the machine shows error E7, turn it off for 10 minutes and run the descaling cycle. Any other error code should be reported to the equipment hotline at extension 4410.

Remote Roles
Corporate staff may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Remote employees receive a one-time $500 home office stipend after completing their probation period, which lasts 90 days.
